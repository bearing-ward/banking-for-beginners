title: Money and your feed
level: starter
summary: Social media is built to make you want things. Learn to spot the pressure, pause before you buy, and keep your own goals in charge.

You open an app to watch one video and twenty minutes later there is a $48 skincare set in your cart. That is not a lack of willpower. Feeds are designed to keep you watching, and ads, creators, and "drops" are designed to make you buy. Knowing how the pressure works makes it easier to say no when you want to.

## Influencers are often paid

When a creator shows a product, they may be getting paid, getting it for free, or earning a cut of every sale through their link or code. That is called a **sponsorship** or **affiliate** deal. The Federal Trade Commission says creators must clearly tell you when they have a connection to a brand, with words like "ad" or "sponsored." See the FTC's [guide for social media influencers](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers).

A paid post is not always a bad recommendation. But it is an ad, so read it like one.

```message A DM after you liked a creator's post
Channel: dm
From: @glowup.daily.deals
Hey!! You have great taste! My code GLOW40 gets you 40% off but it expires at MIDNIGHT. Only 12 sets left!!
! A countdown made to rush you
! "Only 12 left" scarcity you cannot check
! A code that likely pays the sender for each sale
```

## Fake urgency and the real price

"Limited drop," "only 3 left," and countdown timers push you to decide before you think. A discount can also hide the real total once shipping and tax show up at checkout.

```ledger What "40% off" really cost
Skincare set, list price | $80.00
- Discount code, 40% off | $32.00
= Sale price | $48.00
+ Shipping | $7.99
+ Sales tax | $3.36
= Total charged | $59.35
```

The ad said $48. The checkout said $59.35. And if you did not plan to buy skincare today, the "savings" of $32 is really $59.35 spent.

> [!note] Sales tax depends on your state and sometimes your city. A few states have no statewide sales tax, so the same cart can cost a different amount in different places.

## Watch for "pay in 4"

Many checkouts offer **buy now, pay later**: split the price into four payments. It can make a $200 purchase feel like $50. It is still $200, and missed payments can bring late fees. Count the full price, not the first payment.

## Pause before you buy

- **Wait 24 hours** on anything that is not a need. Leave it in the cart. If you still want it tomorrow, it is a real want.
- **Check your goal.** Ask what this pushes back.
- **Unfollow or mute** accounts that make you feel like you are always behind.
- **Turn off saved cards** in apps, so buying takes a few extra steps.

Comparing yourself to people online is hard to avoid. Remember that you see their highlight reel, not their bank balance.

```takeaways
- Creators often earn money when you buy through their link or code; treat those posts as ads.
- Countdowns and "only a few left" are built to rush you.
- Look at the full total with shipping and tax, not the headline price.
- Wait 24 hours before buying a want you saw in your feed.
```
