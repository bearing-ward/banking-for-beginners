Owen is 17 and worked at a grocery store all of 2026. In late January 2027, an envelope arrives from his employer with a **Form W-2** inside. It is the starting point for his first tax return, and it is simpler than he expected.

## When it happens

Your 2026 tax return is filed in early 2027. Employers must send your W-2 by January 31. The filing deadline is in mid-April; the IRS posts the exact date each year. You can file as soon as you have all your forms, and filing early means a refund arrives sooner.

## What to gather

- Every **W-2** from every job you had during the year
- Any **1099** forms, for gig work or interest from a bank
- Your Social Security number
- Your bank's routing number and your account number, for a direct deposit refund
- Whether a parent can claim you as a dependent (ask them)

## Reading your W-2

The W-2 shows a year of pay and withholding. These are the boxes that matter most for a first return.

```table Owen's W-2 for 2026
| Box | What it means | Amount |
| 1 | Wages for federal income tax | $7,800.00 |
| 2 | Federal income tax withheld | $142.00 |
| 3 | Wages for Social Security | $7,800.00 |
| 4 | Social Security tax withheld | $483.60 |
| 5 | Wages for Medicare | $7,800.00 |
| 6 | Medicare tax withheld | $113.10 |
```

Check it against your last pay stub of the year: the year-to-date totals should match. If anything is wrong, ask your employer for a corrected W-2 before you file.

## Where to file for free

You do not need to pay anyone to file a simple return.

- **IRS Free File** offers free guided tax software through [irs.gov](https://www.irs.gov/e-file-do-your-taxes-for-free) for people whose income is under a limit, which covers most teens. Start from the IRS site, not a search ad, so you land on the free version.
- **VITA** (Volunteer Income Tax Assistance) has trained volunteers who prepare returns for free in libraries, schools, and community centers. Find a site through the [IRS VITA page](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/free-tax-return-preparation-for-qualifying-taxpayers).

Most states have their own return too, and many Free File and VITA options include it.

## The box you must not miss

Near the top of Form 1040 is a box that says **someone can claim you as a dependent**. If a parent claims you, check it. It tells the software to use the dependent standard deduction. If you skip it and your parent also claims you, the IRS will see two returns that do not match, and one of you will get a letter.

## Owen's result

Owen is a dependent. His standard deduction is his earned income plus $450, which is $8,250, more than his $7,800 in wages. So his taxable income is $0, and he owes no federal income tax.

```ledger Owen's federal refund
~ Wages, W-2 box 1 | $7,800.00
~ Standard deduction as a dependent | $8,250.00
~ Taxable income | $0.00
Federal income tax withheld, W-2 box 2 | $142.00
- Federal income tax owed | $0.00
= Refund | $142.00
```

The $483.60 of Social Security and $113.10 of Medicare are not refunded. They count toward his future benefits.

## After you file

Choose direct deposit for your refund; it is the fastest and safest way to get it. You can track it with the IRS "Where's My Refund?" tool on irs.gov. Save a copy of your return and your W-2 with your other important papers.

> [!warning] The IRS does not first contact you by text, DM, or phone call about a refund. A message asking you to "verify" your refund through a link is a scam.

```takeaways
- Your W-2 arrives by January 31; the deadline is in mid-April.
- File free with IRS Free File or a VITA volunteer site.
- If a parent can claim you, check "someone can claim you as a dependent."
- Choose direct deposit, and keep a copy of your return.
```
