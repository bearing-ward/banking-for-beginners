---
title: Saving for goals
level: starter
summary: How to save for something specific, like a car, prom, or a phone, by breaking the cost into monthly amounts.
---

Zoe wants three things this year: a used car, prom, and a new phone. Each one feels out of reach when she looks at the full price. Broken into monthly pieces, all three fit in her budget from her job at a frozen yogurt shop.

## Sinking funds

A **sinking fund** is money you set aside a little at a time for an expense you know is coming. The math has two parts:

1. **Cost** of the goal, minus anything you already have.
2. **Divided by the months** until you need it.

```ledger Zoe's prom fund
~ Prom is in 5 months
Dress, shoes, and alterations | $180.00
+ Ticket | $75.00
+ Photos and dinner | $45.00
= Prom total | $300.00
~ $300 ÷ 5 months | $60 a month
```

## Planning several goals at once

Write each goal down with its cost and date, and work out the monthly amount. Then add them up to see whether the total fits.

```table Zoe's goals
| Goal | Cost | Months away | Save each month |
| Prom | $300 | 5 | $60 |
| Phone | $480 | 12 | $40 |
| Used car down payment | $3,000 | 20 | $150 |
```

```ledger Zoe's monthly saving plan
Prom fund | $60.00
+ Phone fund | $40.00
+ Car fund | $150.00
= Total to save each month | $250.00
```

Zoe takes home about $700 a month, so $250 is a big share. If it does not fit, she has three levers: save longer, pick a cheaper version, or drop a goal for now. She decides to look for a $400 phone and push the car out a few months.

## Keep each goal separate

Mixing all your savings in one pile makes it hard to know what is on track. Many banks let you open more than one savings account for free, or split one account into named **buckets** like "Car" or "Prom." Seeing each goal grow is motivating.

Set up an automatic transfer for each one on payday. When prom comes, the money is there, and your emergency fund is untouched.

```takeaways
- A sinking fund turns a big cost into small monthly amounts: cost divided by months.
- Add up all your goals and check that the total fits what you earn.
- Keep each goal in its own account or bucket, and automate the transfers.
```
