---
title: Fake check scams
level: next-step
summary: A check can show up as "available" in your account and still bounce weeks later. When it does, you owe the bank the whole amount.
---

Here is a fact that surprises a lot of adults: when you deposit a check, your bank often lets you use the money within a day or two. That does not mean the check is good. Banks have to make deposited money available quickly, but finding out a check is fake can take weeks.

A **fake check scam** uses that gap. Someone sends you a check, asks you to deposit it, and then asks you to send part of the money somewhere else. By the time the check bounces, your money is gone, and the bank takes the full amount of the check back out of your account.

## How it usually starts

The story changes, but the shape does not: you get a check for more than you are owed, and you are asked to send the difference back or pass it along. Common versions are "secret shopper" jobs, a buyer who overpays for something you are selling, a "grant" or prize, and a remote job that sends a check for "equipment."

```message A secret shopper offer
Channel: email
From: hiring@shopperquest-careers.net
Subject: You're hired: Mystery Shopper, $250 per assignment
Congratulations, Ava! A check for $1,950 is on its way. Deposit it with your banking app, keep $250 as your pay, and use $1,700 to buy gift cards to "test" the store. Send us photos of the card numbers within 24 hours so we can rate your visit.
! You were "hired" without an interview
! A check for more than your pay, with the rest sent back
! Gift card numbers as the way to send money
! A 24-hour deadline before the check can bounce
```

## What happens to the money

Ava had $400 in her checking account. She deposited the check, saw the money become available, and bought the gift cards. Ten days later the bank told her the check was fake.

```ledger Ava's account, start to finish
Ava's balance before | $400.00
+ "Secret shopper" check deposited | $1,950.00
- Gift cards bought and sent | $1,700.00
= Balance once the check "cleared" | $650.00
- Fake check returned unpaid | $1,950.00
- Returned deposit fee | $15.00
= Balance after the check bounces | -$1,315.00
```

Ava's account is now $1,315 below zero. She owes that to the bank, and the gift card money is gone. Her own $400 is gone too. Banks can also close the account of someone who deposits a fake check, even when they were tricked.

> [!warning] "The funds are available" and "the check cleared" are not the same thing. Available means the bank is letting you use the money. It can still come back as fake.

## How to stay safe

- Never accept a check from someone you do not know that is for more than you are owed.
- Never send money back, buy gift cards, or forward funds from a check you received.
- A real employer does not pay you before you work and then ask for some of it back.
- If you are unsure, take the check to your bank and ask them to contact the bank it was written on. Find that bank's number yourself, not from the check.

If you deposited a fake check, call your bank right away, and report it at [reportfraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov). The FTC explains this scam in detail at [consumer.ftc.gov](https://consumer.ftc.gov).

```takeaways
- Money from a check can be available before anyone knows the check is fake.
- If a check bounces, you owe the bank the full amount, plus any fees.
- A check for too much, with a request to send some back, is always a scam.
```
