---
title: Fixed, variable, and once-a-year costs
level: next-step
summary: Some bills are the same every month, some change, and some show up once a year. Plan for all three so none of them catch you off guard.
---

Not every cost behaves the same way. Sorting your costs into three kinds makes your budget far more accurate.

- A **fixed cost** is the same amount every month: rent, a phone plan, a streaming subscription, a car insurance payment.
- A **variable cost** changes from month to month: groceries, gas, eating out, the electric bill.
- An **irregular cost** comes once or twice a year: car registration, a yearly subscription, holiday gifts, prom, a school trip.

## Fixed costs are easy to plan

You know the number in advance, so put it in the budget and you are done. Fixed costs are also hard to cut quickly, which is why it pays to choose them carefully: a lease or a phone contract can lock you in for a year or more.

Eli, 18, drives to his job at a hardware store.

```ledger Eli's fixed monthly costs
Car insurance, his share | $110.00
+ Phone plan | $35.00
+ Music streaming | $12.00
= Fixed costs each month | $157.00
```

## Variable costs: plan with an average

For costs that change, look at the last three months and take the average. Then round up a little for safety.

```ledger Eli's gas, last three months
August | $62.40
+ September | $81.15
+ October | $70.95
= Three-month total | $214.50
~ Average per month | $71.50
~ What he budgets | $75.00
```

Variable costs are where you have the most control. Driving less, packing lunch, or turning the heat down a few degrees changes the number next month.

## Irregular costs: save a little every month

Irregular costs are the ones that wreck budgets, because they are easy to forget until the bill arrives. The fix is a **sinking fund**: add up the year's irregular costs, divide by 12, and set that much aside each month.

```ledger Eli's once-a-year costs
Car registration | $180.00
+ Holiday and birthday gifts | $240.00
+ Two oil changes | $120.00
= Total for the year | $540.00
~ Set aside each month | $45.00
```

When registration comes due, the $180 is already waiting. Car registration fees, inspections, and other vehicle costs vary a lot by state, so check your state's motor vehicle agency for the real numbers.

> [!tip] Keep your sinking fund in a separate savings account or bucket, so it is not mistaken for spending money.

```takeaways
- Fixed costs stay the same; variable costs change; irregular costs come once in a while.
- Budget variable costs at a three-month average, rounded up.
- For irregular costs, divide the yearly total by 12 and save that each month.
- Choose fixed costs carefully, because they are hard to cut later.
```
