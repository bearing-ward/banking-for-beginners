---
title: Splitting bills with roommates
level: next-step
summary: Roommates make rent cheaper, but shared bills cause more arguments than almost anything else. Agree on the split before you move in.
---

A **roommate** can cut your rent by hundreds of dollars a month. Shared bills also mean shared problems: who pays the electric bill, what happens when someone pays late, and whether the person with the bigger room should pay more. Settling it before move-in is much easier than after.

## Split evenly, or by room

Three friends, Jada, Marco, and Lily, rent a three-bedroom apartment. The simplest plan is to split everything three ways.

```ledger The apartment's monthly bills
Rent | $1,800.00
+ Electric | $96.30
+ Internet | $60.00
= Total for the apartment | $1,956.30
~ Each person's share, split three ways | $652.10
```

But Jada's room is bigger and has its own bathroom. So they agree she pays more of the rent, and they split utilities evenly.

```ledger Jada's share, with the bigger room
Rent for the big room | $700.00
+ One third of electric | $32.10
+ One third of internet | $20.00
= Jada pays | $752.10
```

```ledger Marco's share, and Lily's is the same
Rent for a smaller room | $550.00
+ One third of electric | $32.10
+ One third of internet | $20.00
= Marco pays | $602.10
```

Check: $752.10 + $602.10 + $602.10 = $1,956.30, the full total. Any split works if everyone agrees and the numbers add up.

## Who pays the bill, and how

Usually one person's name is on each bill. They pay it, then the others pay them back. A payment app such as Venmo, Zelle, or Cash App makes that fast.

```message A bill reminder in the roommate group chat
Channel: text
From: Lily
Electric bill came in: $96.30, due on the 12th. That's $32.10 each. I'll pay it Monday, please send yours by Sunday!
```

Payment-app transfers usually cannot be undone, so only send money to people you know, and double-check the name before you hit send.

## Put it in writing

Write a short **roommate agreement** and have everyone sign it. Cover:

- Each person's share of rent and each bill
- Who pays which bill, and the deadline to pay them back
- What happens if someone pays late or moves out early
- Shared supplies, like toilet paper and dish soap

> [!warning] If you all sign the same lease, the landlord can often hold any one of you responsible for the full rent if a roommate does not pay. How this works depends on your lease and your state, so read the lease before you sign.

```takeaways
- Agree on how to split rent and bills before you move in.
- Even splits are simplest; splitting rent by room size is also fair if everyone agrees.
- Whoever pays a bill should send the total and each share, with a deadline.
- Write it down, and know that on a shared lease you may be on the hook for the whole rent.
```
