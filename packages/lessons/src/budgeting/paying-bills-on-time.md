title: Paying bills on time
level: starter
summary: Due dates, late fees, and autopay. Paying on time saves money now and protects your credit later.
---

Once you have a phone bill, a car insurance payment, or rent in your name, each one comes with a **due date**: the day the payment has to arrive. Miss it, and you can owe a **late fee**. Miss it by a lot, and it can follow you for years.

## What a late payment costs

The first cost is the fee. Owen, 18, forgot his phone bill while he was busy with finals. His carrier charges a $10 late fee.

```ledger Owen's late phone bill
Monthly phone bill | $45.00
+ Late fee | $10.00
= What he paid | $55.00
```

Ten dollars is annoying. The bigger risk comes later. If a payment on a loan, credit card, or some other accounts is **30 days or more late**, the lender can report it to the credit bureaus. A late payment can stay on your **credit report** for up to seven years and make it harder or more expensive to rent an apartment, get a car loan, or sometimes even set up utilities. You can check your reports for free at [AnnualCreditReport.com](https://www.annualcreditreport.com).

## Know your bills

Make a list of every bill, when it is due, and how you pay it. Many companies let you change your due date, so you can line it up with payday.

```table Owen's bill calendar
| Bill | Amount | Due | How he pays |
| Phone | $45.00 | The 5th | Autopay from checking |
| Car insurance | $110.00 | The 15th | Autopay from checking |
| Streaming | $12.00 | The 20th | Debit card on file |
| Student club dues | $25.00 | October 1 | Pays online by hand |
```

## Autopay helps, with one catch

**Autopay** lets a company pull the payment from your account on the due date. You cannot forget. The catch: if your checking account does not have enough money that day, the payment can fail, or your bank may charge an **overdraft** or returned-payment fee.

- Keep a **buffer**, an extra cushion of money, in checking that you never plan to spend.
- Set autopay for a few days after payday, not before it.
- Still read each bill. Autopay will pay a wrong charge too.

```message A heads-up text from a phone carrier
Channel: text
From: 555-0142
Your bill of $45.00 will be paid automatically on Nov 5 from account ending 3318. Reply HELP for help.
```

Real reminders like this one do not ask you to click a link or share a password. If a "bill" text pushes you to pay through a link or by gift card, it is likely a scam. Log in through the company's own app instead.

> [!tip] Short on money before a due date? Call the company before it is late. Many will move the date or set up a payment plan. Waiting usually costs more.

```takeaways
- Paying late can cost a fee now and hurt your credit for years.
- Keep a list of every bill, its amount, and its due date.
- Use autopay, set a few days after payday, with a buffer in checking.
- If you cannot pay on time, call before the due date.
```
