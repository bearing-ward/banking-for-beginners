---
title: Subscriptions add up
level: starter
summary: Small monthly charges are easy to forget and easy to keep paying. Learn to find them, add them up, and cancel the ones you do not use.
---

A **subscription** is a charge that repeats automatically, usually every month or every year, until you cancel: music, video, games, cloud storage, apps, delivery memberships. Each one is small. That is the problem. Small charges are easy to forget, and they keep coming whether you use the service or not.

## Add them up

Zoe, 17, works at a bakery. She opened her bank app and searched for every charge that repeats.

```ledger Zoe's subscriptions, per month
Music streaming | $11.99
+ Video streaming | $17.99
+ Extra phone storage | $2.99
+ Game subscription | $16.99
+ Workout app | $9.99
= Total per month | $59.95
~ Total per year | $719.40
```

Zoe earns $14.50 an hour. Her subscriptions cost about 50 hours of work a year before taxes. She had not opened the workout app since summer and had not played the game in two months.

## Free trials that turn into charges

Many subscriptions start with a **free trial** that becomes a paid plan unless you cancel in time. The company usually has your card already.

```message A trial reminder email
Channel: email
From: StreamNow Billing
Subject: Your free trial ends tomorrow
Thanks for trying StreamNow Premium! Your 7-day trial ends tomorrow, and your card ending in 4421 will be charged $14.99 a month. Manage your plan in Settings.
! A charge starts automatically if you do nothing
! The cancel option is in settings, not in the email
```

> [!tip] When you start a free trial, set a phone reminder for two days before it ends. Decide then.

## Cut what you do not use

Zoe kept music and video, and canceled the rest.

```ledger What Zoe saves by canceling three
Extra phone storage | $2.99
+ Game subscription | $16.99
+ Workout app | $9.99
= Saved each month | $29.97
~ Saved each year | $359.64
```

That is $359.64 a year she can move to her savings goal. She can always sign up again if she misses something.

## How to cancel

- Cancel in the same place you signed up: the app store, the website, or your phone's subscription settings.
- Look for a confirmation email. Keep it until you see the charges stop.
- If a company keeps charging after you canceled, contact them first, then your bank. You can also report it to the [FTC](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov).

Some states require companies to let you cancel online as easily as you signed up, and to remind you before a free trial converts. The rules vary, so check your state attorney general's website.

```takeaways
- Subscriptions repeat until you cancel, so they are easy to forget.
- Search your bank statement for repeat charges and add them up per year.
- Set a reminder before free trials end.
- Cancel what you do not use; you can sign up again later.
```
