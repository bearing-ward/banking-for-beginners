Your car needs a $400 repair, and payday is two weeks away. A store down the street promises cash today, no credit check. That is a **payday loan**: a small, short-term loan you agree to repay, plus a fee, from your next paycheck. It can be the most expensive way to borrow there is.

**Predatory lending** means loans designed to profit from people who are short on money, with high fees, confusing terms, or pressure to borrow again. Lenders like this count on you not being able to repay on time.

## The real cost of a payday loan

Payday lenders often charge $15 for every $100 you borrow, due in two weeks. That sounds like 15%. But as a yearly rate, the CFPB says a typical two-week loan with that fee works out to an APR of almost 400%. A credit card might charge 24%.

```ledger Cole's $400 payday loan
~ Fee | $15 per $100 borrowed
~ Due | in 2 weeks
Amount borrowed | $400.00
+ Fee | $60.00
= Due on payday | $460.00
```

## The rollover trap

If Cole cannot pay $460 on payday, the lender may offer to **roll over** the loan: he pays the $60 fee, and the $400 is due two weeks later, with another $60 fee. Many borrowers do this again and again.

```ledger Cole rolls the loan over three times
Fee, first two weeks | $60.00
+ Fee, rollover 1 | $60.00
+ Fee, rollover 2 | $60.00
+ Fee, rollover 3 | $60.00
= Fees paid in 8 weeks | $240.00
+ Still owed | $400.00
= Total paid to get out | $640.00
```

After two months, Cole has paid $240 and still owes every dollar he borrowed.

## Other traps that look like help

- **Car title loans** use your car as collateral. Miss a payment and you can lose the car you need for work.
- **Rent-to-own** stores let you take home a TV or phone for weekly payments that can add up to far more than the price.
- **Advance-fee loan scams** promise a loan if you pay a fee first. A real lender never asks you to pay up front with a gift card, wire, or payment app.

```message A loan offer in your DMs
Channel: dm
From: @QuickCashApproved
Congrats! You're PRE-APPROVED for $1,000 today. No credit check! Send a $75 processing fee by gift card to release your funds.
! Guaranteed approval with no credit check
! A fee up front, paid by gift card
! An unknown account reaching out to you first
```

## Better options when you are short

- Ask the shop or company for a payment plan or a few extra days.
- Ask a credit union about a small-dollar loan; many offer short-term loans with far lower rates.
- Ask your employer whether you can get an advance on pay you have already earned.
- Talk to family, and agree on a repayment plan in writing.
- Build an emergency fund, even $25 a paycheck, so the next surprise does not need a loan.

Rules for payday and title loans depend on your state, and some states ban them. Your state attorney general's website explains what is allowed. Report scams at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov), and read the [FTC guide to advance-fee loan scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-advance-fee-loans).

```takeaways
- A $15 per $100 two-week payday loan works out to an APR of almost 400%.
- Rolling over a payday loan piles on fees without lowering what you owe.
- A lender that asks for a fee up front, especially by gift card, is a scam.
- Ask for a payment plan, try a credit union, or use an emergency fund instead.
```
