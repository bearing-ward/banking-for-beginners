Nina found a used car for $15,000. She has saved $2,000 for a **down payment**, the cash you pay up front. She will borrow the other $13,000 with an **auto loan**, a loan where the car itself is the **collateral**: if you stop paying, the lender can take the car back. That is called **repossession**.

Dealers like to ask, "What monthly payment can you afford?" A better question is: what will this car cost me in total?

```ledger Nina's car loan
~ APR | 8%
~ Monthly payment | $263.59 × 60 months
Car price | $15,000.00
- Down payment | $2,000.00
= Amount borrowed | $13,000.00
+ Interest over 5 years | $2,815.40
+ Down payment | $2,000.00
= Total cost of the car | $17,815.40
```

## Longer loans cost more

Stretching a loan over more months makes each payment smaller, which is why longer loans are tempting. But you pay interest for longer, so the total goes up. Here is Nina's same $13,000 at 8% APR over three lengths.

```table Borrowing $13,000 at 8% APR
| Loan length | Monthly payment | Total interest | Total repaid |
| 36 months | $407.37 | $1,665.32 | $14,665.32 |
| 60 months | $263.59 | $2,815.40 | $15,815.40 |
| 72 months | $227.93 | $3,410.96 | $16,410.96 |
```

The 72-month loan saves Nina $35.66 a month compared with 60 months, but costs $595.56 more in interest. A long loan also means you can end up owing more than the car is worth, since cars lose value every year.

## Your rate depends on your credit

Your **APR** depends on your credit history, the car's age, and the lender. A first-time buyer with no history often gets a higher rate. At 12% instead of 8%, Nina's 60-month payment would be $289.18 and her interest $4,350.80, which is $1,535.40 more. Get a preapproved rate from a bank or credit union before you shop, so you can compare it with the dealer's offer. If you are under 18, you cannot sign a loan on your own, so a parent or guardian would have to be involved.

## The payment is not the whole cost

A car comes with bills every month on top of the loan. Lenders require full insurance coverage on a financed car, and insurance for new teen drivers is expensive.

```ledger Nina's real monthly car budget
Loan payment | $263.59
+ Insurance | $180.00
+ Gas | $90.00
+ Maintenance and repairs, set aside | $50.00
= Monthly cost of the car | $583.59
```

> [!tip] Before you buy, run the numbers in the loan calculator beside this lesson. Try a bigger down payment and a shorter loan, and get a real insurance quote for the exact car.

The [CFPB's auto loan guide](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/auto-loans/) walks through shopping for a loan.

```takeaways
- Judge a car loan by its total cost, not the monthly payment.
- A longer loan lowers the payment but raises the interest you pay.
- A bigger down payment and a better APR both save real money.
- Add insurance, gas, and repairs before you decide what you can afford.
```
